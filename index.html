01About

I'm Omar, a high-school student in Waterloo, Ontario who ships production-grade software. Most of what I build sits where markets meet code: Solana programs that enforce custody rules on-chain, trading systems with honest backtests and hard risk limits, and AI agents that make real decisions under real constraints.

I work across the whole stack, from Rust and Anchor programs to TypeScript APIs, React front ends, browser extensions and mobile apps. I care about the unglamorous parts too: ledgers that reconcile, tests, explicit failure modes and documentation that says exactly what the code does.
